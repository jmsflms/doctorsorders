An excuse to get together

More than a Saturday morning 5K.

Sea air, coastal and countryside runs, fantastic pubs and a big house for the whole crew. Whitstable is around an hour from London by fast train.

This is not a coached retreat or training camp. It's a self-catered, self-organised whole-house weekend for existing running groups and their friends.

Non-runners welcome. Run, walk, explore Whitstable or stay cozy at home.

Whether you want a cold plunge in the sea or relax in our hot tub, this is a fun weekend for friends who met while running.

Three possible weekends

Pick your kind of weekend.

Packed with possibilities, not a timetable. Mix, match or skip anything you like. Checkout is 11am Sunday.

EXAMPLE ITINERARY 1

The Social One

Friday: Arrive, settle in and enjoy dinner together.

Saturday: Whitstable parkrun, coffee, a pub lunch, then a seaside walk or another run. Back home for a proper dinner party.

Sunday: Early easy run and an optional goodbye sea swim.

EXAMPLE ITINERARY 2

The Long Run One

Friday: Arrive, gather for roll call, then run to the pub for dinner!

Saturday: 5K parkrun, coffee and seaside wandering, then a leisurely 10K towards The Sportsman. Afternoon sauna (book separately), followed by dinner.

Sunday: Early coastal run and an optional goodbye sea swim.

EXAMPLE ITINERARY 3

The Mixed Crew

Friday: Meet everyone over dinner at the house.

Saturday: Some head out running while others explore Whitstable, the shops and the beach. Meet for lunch, then an optional 16K for the keen runners while everyone else hits the hot tub.

Sunday: Early coastal walk, coffee and goodbyes.

A house, not a bootcamp

Run five miles. Or just meet us at the pub.

Stay together at The Old Doctor's Manor House. Five en-suite bedrooms, generous shared spaces, kitchen, dining room, cinema room and garden hot tub.

Some will run every day. Others might never put their trainers on. Everyone is part of the weekend.
